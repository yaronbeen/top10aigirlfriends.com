# Learnings

## 2026-07-13 Research Snapshot

Top competing review pages emphasize rankings by use case, not just a single overall winner. Common evaluation axes: conversation quality, image realism, memory, customization, voice/video, privacy, price, and free-tier usefulness.

Reddit sentiment found during research repeatedly warns that novelty fades quickly if memory and conversational consistency are weak. The site should not over-index on visuals alone.

Candy.ai's public page leans into a dark app-like interface, character discovery, image/video/voice, privacy, discreet billing, and a broad character catalog.

## 2026-07-14 Implementation Learnings

Replicate GPT Image 2 repeatedly flagged prompts containing direct adult/relationship wording. Safe abstract/editorial prompts around chat interfaces, privacy, memory, app comparison, and luxury tech visuals generated successfully and produced usable site assets.

For programmatic SEO pages, noindex long-tail pages that are structurally repetitive and keep only stronger keyword/editorial pages indexed. This preserves internal linking without exposing thin pages to Google.

Ratings should be computed from score axes, not manually entered. Manual ratings drifted from the published methodology during review.
